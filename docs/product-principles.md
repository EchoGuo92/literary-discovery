# Product Principles

## Product job

Help a reader encounter a work they would not have searched for, understand enough to make a real reading decision, and retain what matters without turning reading into a task system.

## Home

**Home = Encounter + Decision.**

It should answer:

- What is this?
- Why is it worth stopping for?
- Do I know enough to decide whether I want to read it?
- Why did it appear from my current entry point?

Canonical information flow:

1. Concrete premise
2. “这本书有什么特别”
3. Reading Mood
4. “与你的联系”
5. Writer entry
6. Want / Read / Not Interested

### Copy responsibilities

- **Premise** — what is happening?
- **Special** — why is the work distinctive on its own?
- **Reading Mood** — what does reading it feel like?
- **Your Connection** — why did this work appear from this anchor now?

“Your Connection” should not become a second synopsis.

## Recommendation composition

The three recommendations are not a ranked similarity list.

- **Bridge** preserves more.
- **Expansion** preserves one meaningful pleasure while changing important variables.
- **Explore** can travel far; it only needs one credible handrail.

The reader should feel the widening without seeing these internal labels.

## Writer

Writer is supporting information only:

1. Writer Signal
2. Context
3. Save Writer

No mandatory writer-to-writer connection module. A compelling relationship is editorial material, not a reason to create a permanent architecture.

## Library

Library contains only things the reader deliberately retains:

- Want to Read
- Read
- Saved Writers
- Reflections

No stats, streaks, progress, ratings, achievements, or taste dashboard.

## Reflection

Open input. No fixed questions, follow-up interrogation, completion state, or reminders.

## Search

Known desire only. Search is not a recommendation feed.

## Simplification rule

> Do not turn a beautiful exceptional case into a mandatory module.

Before adding a feature, ask:

> Will this make the Home recommendation more convincing?

If not, it does not belong in this MVP.
